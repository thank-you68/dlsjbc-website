Who We Are

Rooted in Faith. Driven by Purpose.

For generations, DLSJBC has formed graduates who lead with conscience, compassion, and competence — the Lasallian way.

Mission

Why We Exist

To provide accessible, values-based education that forms competent, God-loving, and service-oriented citizens.

Vision

Where We're Headed

A learning community recognized for academic excellence, integrity, and transformative service to the wider community.

Core Values

What We Stand For

Faith, Service, and Communion in Mission — guiding every classroom, every partnership, every learner.

History

Our Journey

Built on the shared legacy of the De La Salle Brothers and the Salesians of Don Bosco, educating with heart and mind.
