Admissions Made Simple

Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

When does enrollment open for the next school year?+

Enrollment for A.Y. 2026–2027 is open now through the Online Admission Portal, with priority slots for early applicants.

Does DLSJBC offer scholarships?+

Yes — academic, athletic, and need-based scholarships are available. Details are provided during the admissions interview.

Is there a Student Portal for grades and schedules?+

All enrolled students receive access to the Student Portal for grades, schedules, and announcements.

Can I schedule a campus visit before applying?+

Absolutely. Reach out through the Contact section below and our admissions team will arrange a guided tour.
