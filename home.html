Admissions Made Simple

Four simple steps stand between you and a place among our community of faith, service, and excellence.

Step 1

Inquire

Reach out through our Contact page or visit the campus to learn about available programs.

Step 2

Submit Requirements

Complete the Online Admission Portal and submit required documents such as report cards and birth certificate.

Step 3

Take the Entrance Assessment

Applicants take a placement exam and, where applicable, an admissions interview.

Step 4

Enroll & Begin

Once accepted, complete enrollment and gain access to the Student Portal.
